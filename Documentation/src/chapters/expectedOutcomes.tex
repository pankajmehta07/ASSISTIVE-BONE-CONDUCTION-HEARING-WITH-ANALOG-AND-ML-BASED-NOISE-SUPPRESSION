\chapter{EXPECTED OUTCOMES}
After completion, we expect to have a working prototype with the following characteristics. Steady background noise such as a fan located one meter away will be reduced substantially through analog filters, achieving a noticeable drop in loudness. Impulsive noises like a door slam or a metal object dropping will be either muted or attenuated to near inaudibility in more than nine out of ten occurrences. The end-to-end delay (latency) from microphone input to bone conduction output will remain below 15 milliseconds. Speech from a person standing two meters away in a moderately noisy environment will be clearly understood, as verified by a proposed qualitative test with five listeners. Finally, the system will draw only modest current from a typical small battery, enabling continuous operation for several hours on a single charge.
